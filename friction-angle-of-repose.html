What is the angle of repose?

The angle of repose is the smallest angle of inclination of a plane with the horizontal at which a body placed on it just begins to slide down.

tan θᵣ = μₛ

At the limiting condition, static friction reaches its maximum value. If the angle increases beyond θᵣ, the block slides.

Forces on the block

Weight (mg): acts vertically downward.

Normal reaction (N): acts perpendicular to the plane.

Static friction (fₛ): acts up the plane, opposing the tendency to slide.

mg sin θ = fₛ
N = mg cos θ
At limiting state: μₛ = tan θᵣ

Try these observations

1. Start below the angle of repose. What keeps the block from sliding?

2. Increase the angle until the block just starts moving. Note this critical angle.

3. Increase the angle further. What happens to the block?
